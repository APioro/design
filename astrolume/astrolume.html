Astrolume

A brand concept for an astronomical community for amateur stargazers, designed to bridge curiosity and knowledge through engaging visuals, accessible insights, and a shared passion for the cosmos. Astrolume differentiates itself by valuing the wonder and beauty of stargazing as much as scientific accuracy. Its mission is to inspire people to look up at the sky again, rather than down at their phones.
